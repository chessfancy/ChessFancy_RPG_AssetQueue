# Chapter 2 Cutscene Reference Plan

Status: approved story direction; references to be produced after screenplay lock.

## 1. Red Map cutscene

Purpose: show the highland shrinking over years without requiring character-consistent video acting.

### Reference A — historical clean highland map

Must show, without spoiling Xương Cuồng:
- Trạm Giữ Tuyến region
- former villages / terraced agriculture
- Sơn Tinh shrine
- patrol/build routes
- Sườn Núi Từng Bước
- upper ancient highland region

No Xương Cuồng label or boss imagery.

### Reference B — current red-marked map

Same geography and framing as Reference A, but:
- upper settlements crossed/marked out in layers
- old routes partially faded/lost
- currently usable lower area visibly compressed

### Video direction

Prefer environment/object animation over visible full-body people:
- camera drift over map
- pointer / marker movement if reliable
- red marks appearing in historical layers
- close-up of one or two named/recognizable settlement zones being crossed out

Offscreen NPC dialogue is allowed during the cutscene:
- “Nhà tôi ở đây.”
- “Nhà tôi cao hơn một đoạn nữa.”

After the cutscene, control returns and those NPCs provide optional free-explore lines about the old Sơn Tinh shrine and the belief that the guardian may be angry.

## 2. Sơn Tinh appearance

Existing accepted Sơn Tinh intro video remains the source for his first full manifestation unless screenplay staging later requires a new edit. After video, a real exploration actor must exist in the map; do not continue named dialogue from an invisible/offscreen Sơn Tinh.

Canonical dialogue correction:

`Ta chưa từng ruồng bỏ họ.`

The old “bậc đá / thêm thông tin / kế hoạch vẫn cần” teaching sequence is deleted from canon.

## 3. Xương Cuồng final cinematic

Prerequisites before generation:
- user-approved Xương Cuồng boss canonical reference (`boss_manual`)
- locked Sơn Tinh canonical reference
- approved Map 8 / Cao Nguyên Cổ Mộc environment reference
- screenshot/reference of boss after the party has forced the main root system free from the ground

Generation plan: five continuous Veo 3.1 Lite clips, 8 seconds each, linked by the previous clip's ending frame/video reference where supported.

Continuity beats:
1. Sơn Tinh fully manifests; Xương Cuồng turns to fight him.
2. Xương Cuồng attacks with major roots; Sơn Tinh defends the lower valley with rising stone.
3. Prolonged struggle; Sơn Tinh uses terrain to separate roots from the earth and identifies the loosened root core.
4. Sơn Tinh gains advantage; stone anchors force the root system fully out of the living highland.
5. Sơn Tinh expels/seals Xương Cuồng away from the plateau's địa mạch; normal wind and a small water flow return.

Do not kill Xương Cuồng with a gore/destruction beat. Preferred resolution is expulsion from the địa mạch plus sealing/containment.

The 5×8s generation gives ~40s raw footage. Final edit may trim/overlap junctions to roughly 30–35 seconds while preserving continuity.
