## Design rule
Prefer enemies that can still make sense even if Chapter 4's exact plot changes. Their common denominator is old battlefield material + wind + obsolete military behavior, not a specific scripted scene.

## Current Muse assignment
The eight **new wave** identities receive canonical-reference candidate requests first. Full idle/attack/hurt/KO/special/VFX jobs are intentionally deferred until later selection so unused candidates do not consume animation production unnecessarily.
