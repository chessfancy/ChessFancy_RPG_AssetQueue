# Chapter 3–5 Rewrite Roadmap

Status: backlog created after Chapter 2 world-building audit. Do not generate final connected-map sets for these chapters until each story is locked.

## Chapter 3 — Vực Biển Tàn Cuộc / Thủy Tinh

Decision: `STORY REWRITE FROM FOUNDATION`.

Current implementation starts underwater and introduces Thủy Tinh too late; it is not the target screenplay.

Locked direction already known:
- Chapter 2 ends at the old route overlooking the sea.
- Chapter 3 Map 1 is the cliff edge / Mép Vực.
- Thủy Tinh appears early, before underwater traversal.
- Thủy Tinh is male.
- He clarifies that there is no simplistic Sơn Tinh–Thủy Tinh feud framing.
- He opens a controlled water path down the cliff; the party can breathe while remaining inside the flow.
- Control returns for the descent.

Existing Chapter 3 art inventory should be audited before regeneration. Current source material includes multiple 1672×941 environment candidates and underwater prop sheets; classify each as KEEP / REPURPOSE / REFERENCE_ONLY / REGENERATE after the new screenplay and route graph are locked.

Story-room questions before map production:
- why Thủy Tinh is at the cliff now
- his current relationship/history with Sơn Tinh
- what underwater community/problem the party actually enters
- which existing Seahorse/Jelly/Crab/Octopus concepts still serve the new story
- where Guardian Thủy Tinh unlock belongs in the dramatic structure
- how Chapter 3 exit naturally points into Chapter 4

## Chapter 4 — Chiến Trường Gió Nổi / Thánh Gióng

Preserve as candidates, not canon:

Story-room questions:

- how “play the position actually on the board” is dramatized through events rather than speeches
- how Thánh Gióng participates before/facing the chapter climax instead of appearing only as a late reward

## Chapter 5 — Minh Châu / Long Quân / Finale

Decision: `REWRITE FINALE FROM FOUNDATION, RETAIN USEFUL CONCEPTS`.

The old tower/replay premise, Ma Vương, replay ghosts and clockwork enemies may be retained only if they survive the new story-room review.

Story-room questions:
- whether the Clock Tower remains the final location
- how the party actually finds Minh Châu
- what Minh Châu has experienced while separated from them
- why the final antagonist is holding/stopping her
- when and why Long Quân enters the story
- how previously met Guardians contribute to the finale without replacing the party
- how the ending avoids explaining the theme directly in speeches
- what returns the party and Minh Châu to the real world

## Vertical production rule

For each chapter:
1. story / screenplay lock
2. route graph and map count
3. reuse audit
4. connected world/map art
5. canonical references / props / landmarks
6. enemy/boss/guardian AssetQueue production
7. Sol/Hermes integration and collision/event wiring
8. runtime QC and playable chapter witness
9. only then move to the next chapter
