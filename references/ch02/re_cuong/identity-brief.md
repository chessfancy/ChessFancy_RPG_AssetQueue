# Rễ Cuồng — identity brief

Purpose: new Chapter 2 regular enemy identity candidate.

Story role:
- A dangerous living-root threat in the later Chapter 2 root zone.
- More aggressive and physically forceful than Mộc Linh, but still an ordinary enemy below Xương Cuồng / Mộc Tinh.
- Child-friendly fantasy threat: no gore, body horror, skulls or disturbing anatomy.

Visual direction:
- Original Vietnamese-fantasy JRPG plant/wood creature.
- Strong readable silhouette built from twisted roots, bark knots and restrained leaves/vines.
- Clear front/attack direction so battle poses remain readable; avoid an amorphous pile of roots.
- Warm bark/earth tones, muted greens, restrained amber/gold disturbance accents if useful.
- No modern objects, text, UI, watermark or scenery.
- Canonical battle reference faces RIGHT, full body/root mass visible with generous margin.

Use `style-bibles/chapter02/README.md` and `references/style/ch02/` for chapter/style language. Keep it visually distinct from Mộc Linh and clearly below the boss Xương Cuồng / Mộc Tinh in scale and complexity.
