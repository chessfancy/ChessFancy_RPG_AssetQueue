# Gấu Rừng — identity brief

Purpose: new Chapter 2 regular enemy identity candidate.

Story role:
- A forest/mountain bear encountered after Sơn Tinh is unlocked.
- It belongs naturally to the highland biome and is disturbed/agitated by the root-zone escalation rather than being an imported fantasy beast.
- Child-friendly JRPG enemy: sturdy and threatening enough for battle, never gore/horror.

Visual direction:
- Original Vietnamese-fantasy JRPG creature design.
- Compact, sturdy bear silhouette readable at small battle scale.
- Natural mountain/forest anatomy with stylized chibi proportions; avoid humanoid armor-heavy design.
- Earth/bark browns, restrained forest greens, subtle root/leaf disturbance cues are allowed.
- No modern accessories, text, UI, watermark or scenery.
- Canonical battle reference faces RIGHT, full body visible with generous margin.

Use `style-bibles/chapter02/README.md` and `references/style/ch02/` for chapter/style language. Do not copy Pawn Wall or Knight Wild identity/anatomy.
