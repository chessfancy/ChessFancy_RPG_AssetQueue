# Chapter 4 — Chiến Trường Gió Nổi — Enemy Style Bible

Status: candidate-production art direction, NOT story canon.

## Core visual idea
Chapter 4 crosses an old battlefield where terrain, wind and surviving war objects no longer match the orders they once obeyed. Enemy designs should feel like remnants of a battle system still trying to execute old commands after the battlefield has changed.

## Visual language
- child-friendly Vietnamese fantasy JRPG
- hand-painted 2D character rendering consistent with the global style bible
- side-view battle readability first; canonical enemy reference faces RIGHT
- full body / complete object silhouette, generous safe margin
- old bamboo, dark lacquered wood, woven cloth, rope, bronze/iron fittings, weathered lamellar/scale-like armor forms
- wind may animate cloth, tassels, ribbons, dust or empty equipment, but do not turn every enemy into the same blue tornado
- weathered and old, not rotten gore-horror
- no modern military equipment, firearms, vehicles or industrial machinery
- no text, UI, labels, watermark, detached prop clutter

## Tone
The battlefield should feel strange and persistent rather than evil for evil's sake. Many enemies can be empty gear, constructs, decoys, weapon spirits or wind-driven remnants that continue an obsolete behavior.

## Anti-duplication
Existing/legacy Chapter 4 candidates already include:
- bamboo_soldier
- iron_horse_young
- drum_ogre
- general_forgot

New candidates should add distinct silhouettes and combat roles rather than repaint those four.

## Production policy
- New entries in this wave are regular-enemy candidate identities using enemy_auto.
- A successful canonical reference does NOT make the enemy story canon.
- Sol/story team may use or discard any candidate later.
- Do not generate full animation bundles until a later executable request selects the candidate for animation production.
